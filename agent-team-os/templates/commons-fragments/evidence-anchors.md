## Evidence Anchors

- Every cross-agent relay that asserts a fact must carry an evidence anchor: a source path, a content hash, or raw command/tool output.
- **No artifact = no conclusion.** If you cannot point to a readable artifact, report the claim as unverified — not as done.
- Before reporting completion, re-read the artifact and check it against the claim (name, content, hash where available).
- Do not paraphrase numbers, identifiers, or timestamps from memory. Copy them exactly from the source, or state that you do not have them.
- Summaries do not replace anchors. Link or quote the source alongside any summary.
- Report failures honestly and as-is: what was attempted, what failed, what the system actually returned. A failed or empty result is still a result.
- Never invent a value to fill a gap. Say it is unknown, and say what would be needed to obtain it.
